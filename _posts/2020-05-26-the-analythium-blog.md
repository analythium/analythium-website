Welcome to the Analythium Blog! In this is an introductory post where we explain what you can expect to find on our blog.

# Who we are

[Analythium](https://analythium.io/?ref=blog.analythium.io) is a team of data scientists, domain experts, and business professionals. We help organizations to become data driven by facilitating digital transformation and providing advanced analytics solutions to improve  business functions and enable better decisions in less time.

We leverage domain expertise to scope data problems, aggregate disparate data sources, and work across silos in complex organizations, democratizing data enterprise-wide. This process improves business functionality, enable pinpointed data accuracy, reduces enterprise risk obligations, and saves millions.

![The Analythium Blog](https://www.analythium.io/favicon.ico)

# What to expect from the blog

We know that your time is precious, therefore we keep our blog posts short and to the point. We value focus and simplicity in how we communicate complex topics. Focus means that we clearly define *what's in it for you* at the start and take you there through the shortest path without unnecessary meandering. Simplicity means that we do not use jargon or complicate things just to show off.

We have planned content that will present real-world case studies and applications to demonstrate how we use interactive visualization, and the power of statistics and data science to turn challenging data problems into a business advantage.

We have accumulated a wealth of knowledge about building production-ready applications and the supporting infrastructure. In some of the posts, we will share our experiences and the know-how to help collectively raise the bar for boutique software and consulting.

We will also keep the readers up-to-date on news, upcoming events, conferences, and webinars.

# Until next time

If you want to make sure you don't miss any of our future posts, sign up to our newsletter. Thanks for reading, and see you next time!
