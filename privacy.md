## Who we are and what this covers

Analythium Solutions Inc. (“Analythium”, “we”, “us” or “our”) operates this website. This policy describes information that may be processed when you visit the site, contact us, or follow links to third-party services.

## Information you provide

If you contact us through the website form, you may provide your name, email address, company, and message. The form is hosted and processed by Reform; information you submit is sent to Reform and made available to us so we can respond to your inquiry. Please review [Reform’s privacy information](https://www.reform.app/privacy) for its handling of submitted data.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through the contact form unless we have agreed on an appropriate secure method.

## Information collected when you visit

This website uses browser local storage to remember your selected display theme and whether you dismissed the announcement banner. These preferences are stored in your browser and are not used by this site to identify you.

Our current shared metadata template does not load an analytics script. The infrastructure that serves the site or third-party resources embedded on a page may process technical information, such as a request’s IP address, to deliver, protect, or troubleshoot those services. Their own privacy policies apply to that processing.

## How we use information

We use information you provide to review and respond to inquiries, communicate with you, and operate our business. We may use or disclose information where reasonably necessary to protect our rights, address security or abuse, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

## Service providers and international processing

We rely on service providers, including Reform for the website contact form, to operate parts of our services. Those providers may process information on our behalf and may store or process it in locations outside your province, territory, or country. Their processing is subject to their terms and privacy practices.

We do not sell personal information submitted through this website. We may disclose information when required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

## Retention and security

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention by third-party providers is also subject to their policies. We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Your choices and rights

You can clear or block local storage through your browser settings; doing so may prevent the site from remembering your theme or announcement preference. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.

## Links to other websites

This site may link to websites or services operated by others. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information to them.

## Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as our practices or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the date above.
