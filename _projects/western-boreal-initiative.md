The Western Boreal Initiative brings together ecological models, data, and domain expertise to help understand how interacting disturbances and a changing climate may shape Canada's Western Boreal forests.

The work considers multiple forest values—including woodland caribou, other species at risk, timber, carbon, and downstream economic values—to support collaborative planning and explore potential trade-offs.
