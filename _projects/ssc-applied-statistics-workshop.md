## Motivation

Applied statistics increasingly needs to reach audiences through interactive media rather than static reports alone. Statisticians and applied scientists benefit from software-engineering practices that support reusable code, working prototypes, shorter stakeholder feedback loops, and applications that can scale and perform reliably.

## The project

At the Statistical Society of Canada’s 2022 Annual Meeting, Analythium's team delivered a six-hour accreditation workshop titled “Delivering applied statistics from concept to production.”

The workshop covered shaping a question and dataset into a modeling workflow; building command-line interfaces and prototypes;deploying applications to cloud-hosted virtual machines; and improving performance by separating application logic from its presentation layer.

## Our results

Participants received a practical path from exploratory analysis to reusable workflows, interactive prototypes, containerized deployment, and decoupled APIs. Participants were provided with access to the workshop slides and hands-on exercises for continued learning and reuse.
